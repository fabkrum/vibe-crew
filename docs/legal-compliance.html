Legal & Compliance Patterns

Compliance patterns that VibeCrew agents enforce during planning and code review. Every pattern references the applicable regulation, documents the penalty for non-compliance, and provides actionable implementation guidance for DACH-region SaaS applications.

Legal PagesConsent ManagementData RightsAccessibility Compliance

Legal Pages

Mandatory legal pages that every commercial website in the EU/DACH region must provide — Impressum, privacy policy, and terms of service.

Impressum Page

Legal notice page required in DACH region. Must include legal entity name, address, email, VAT ID, and trade register entry. Accessible within 2 clicks from every page.

Legal Requirement

TMG §5 (DE), ECG §5 (AT)

Penalty

Abmahnung fines up to €50,000

Privacy Policy

Data processing disclosure. What data is collected, legal basis, third parties, retention periods, and user rights. Linked from every page footer.

Legal Requirement

GDPR Art. 13/14

Penalty

Up to 4% annual revenue or €20M

Terms of Service

Usage terms for the service. Covers scope, payment, liability, and cancellation (14-day Widerrufsrecht for EU consumers). Referenced during signup.

Legal Requirement

AGB-Recht (DACH B2C)

Penalty

Unfair terms are void, potential damages

Consent Management

Mechanisms for collecting, recording, and enforcing user consent before processing personal data or loading third-party scripts.

Cookie Consent Banner

Consent mechanism for non-essential cookies. Block non-essential cookies before consent. Reject must be as easy as Accept. Granular categories. Log consent for auditing.

Legal Requirement

GDPR + ePrivacy (TTDSG)

Penalty

Up to €300,000 per violation

Docs →

Consent Logging

Auditable record of user consent. Store timestamp, consent version, choices made, and IP address. Retain for duration of processing plus statute of limitations.

Legal Requirement

GDPR Art. 7(1)

Penalty

Unable to prove lawful basis for processing

Third-Party Script Gating

Block analytics and marketing scripts until consent. No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, HubSpot, or Intercom before explicit consent. Load scripts dynamically after consent callback.

Legal Requirement

GDPR + TTDSG

Penalty

Up to €300,000

Data Rights

User rights under GDPR that your application must support — data access, portability, rectification, and erasure.

Data Deletion Request

Mechanism for users to request data erasure. Account deletion flow, data anonymization where deletion is not feasible, confirmation within 30 days.

Legal Requirement

GDPR Art. 17 (Right to Erasure)

Penalty

Up to 4% annual revenue

Accessibility Compliance

Legal obligations for digital accessibility under the European Accessibility Act (EAA) and German BFSG, effective June 2025.

Accessibility Statement

Declaration of accessibility conformance. Conformance level (WCAG 2.1 AA), known issues, remediation timeline, and feedback mechanism.

Legal Requirement

EAA/BFSG (June 2025)

Penalty

Up to €100,000 or 4% revenue
